Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Welcome to Wearzeno ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use the Wearzeno mobile application (the "App").

1. No Data Collection

Wearzeno is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the App — including clothing photos, wardrobe data, style preferences, and outfit ratings — remains exclusively on your device.

2. Camera Access

The App requests access to your camera solely to allow you to photograph clothing items for your wardrobe. Photos are processed locally on your device and stored in the App's private document directory. No photos are ever transmitted off your device.

3. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library solely to allow you to select existing images of clothing items. We read selected images only and do not access your entire photo library beyond what you explicitly choose.

4. Location Data

The App requests your approximate location solely to fetch current weather conditions from the Open-Meteo API. Location data is used in real-time to retrieve weather information and is never stored, logged, or transmitted to any third party other than the weather API for the sole purpose of returning weather data. You may decline location access; the App will function without weather features.

5. Local Storage

All App data is stored locally on your device using:

  • AsyncStorage — for preferences, settings, and generated outfit data
  • File System — for clothing images stored in the App's Documents directory

This data is not accessible to other applications and is removed when you delete the App.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and use cookies for ad personalization. For details, see Google's Advertising Policy. The only other external network request is to the Open-Meteo weather API (open-meteo.com) for weather data.

7. No Accounts or Authentication

The App does not require user registration, login, or any form of authentication. No email addresses, names, or personal identifiers are collected.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the App is compliant with COPPA and similar regulations.

9. Data Storage Compliance

The App complies with Apple's iOS Data Storage Guidelines. User-generated content (clothing photos) is stored in the Documents directory and is backed up by default. No cached or temporary data is stored in the Documents directory.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
